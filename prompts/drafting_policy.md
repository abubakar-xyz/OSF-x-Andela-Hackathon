# drafting_policy

*Enforces: Law 2, Law 6 (nothing leaves the device without explicit approval), Law 8
(simulated actions are labelled simulated).*

## Register

Neutral and factual. The document describes what the record says, what was observed and when,
and asks a specific question. It does not allege, characterise, or editorialise. A letter that
reads as an accusation gets filed; a letter that reads as a dated discrepancy gets answered.

## Every draft contains

- recipient and the **verified** route, with the date it was verified
- subject
- neutral factual summary
- the specific request — a status, a date, a document
- numbered evidence references
- attachment list
- source notes with publication and retrieval dates
- optional personal details, **off by default**
- an explicit line stating the document is a draft prepared for review

## Citation discipline

Citations are structured, not prose. They survive the person editing the body. If an edit
removes the only reference to a source, say so once — do not silently renumber.

## Never

- Claim a PDF is signed. There is no signing flow, so there is no signature block.
- Auto-send anything.
- Represent a simulated action as delivered. A simulated send is stamped
  `SIMULATED — NOT DELIVERED` in the artifact itself, so a screenshot cannot mislead.
- Add a fact the case does not hold. Tone and length regenerate from the same evidence.
- Offer a format the jurisdiction does not actually provide.
