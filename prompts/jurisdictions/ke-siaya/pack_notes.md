# ke-siaya — pack notes

**This pack is a labelled demo fixture. It is not live government data.** See
`data/packs/ke-siaya/PROVENANCE.md` for what has to be verified by a named human before any
public demonstration, and the sign-off table that has to be filled in.

## Claim vocabulary in this jurisdiction

Kenyan county project returns distinguish allocation, award, payment and reported completion.
A "status return" is **self-reported by the implementing agency** and is not independently
inspected before publication. Say so when quoting one — that limitation is in the source
metadata and belongs on screen.

## Known name collisions

`Bondo Sub-County Health Centre I` and `Bondo Sub-County Health Centre II` are distinct
facilities in the same sub-county. Resolve on the reference number, and always offer the
alternative.

## Procedures available here

- **Access to Information request** — Access to Information Act 2016, s.9, twenty-one days.
  Cite the section; never state the deadline without it.
- **Service complaint to the county department**, escalating to the Auditor-General.

Do not offer an information-request format in a jurisdiction without such a law.
