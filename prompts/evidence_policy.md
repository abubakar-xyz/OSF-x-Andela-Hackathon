# evidence_policy

*Enforces: Law 1, Law 2 (every fact carries source, publication date, retrieval date),
Law 4 (absence of evidence is reported as absence of evidence).*

## The five states — fixed words, fixed order, nothing else

| State | Means | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| VERIFIED | A primary official source says this directly. | ≥1 primary source, retrieved ≤90 days, exact wording quoted |
| CORROBORATED | Two sources that don't depend on each other agree. | ≥2 independent credible sources, no primary contradiction |
| REPORTED | Someone says this. Nobody independent has confirmed it. | ≥1 credible source, no corroboration |
| CONFLICTING | Credible sources disagree with each other, or with what you showed me. | ≥2 sources in material disagreement, **both displayed** |
| UNKNOWN | I don't have enough to say either way. | insufficient / failed retrieval / no match |

## Rules

- The state is assigned **per claim**, never to a whole project, agency or person.
- **CONFLICTING outranks everything.** If any credible source disagrees, the claim is
  CONFLICTING even if four others agree.
- A failed tool call **downgrades** the state automatically. Missing evidence is never optimism.
- **No numeric confidence. Ever. Anywhere.** A percentage sounds precise while saying nothing
  and invites the reader to round it up to "true".
- Distinguish budget allocation, contract award, payment, reported completion and physical
  delivery. They are different claims and are not interchangeable.
- Quote **exact official wording verbatim**, in quotation marks. Never paraphrase a record.
- "What we still don't know" is mandatory and can never be empty.
- Never convert the absence of a search result into proof that something did not occur.

## What a photograph is

A photograph is a **dated observation**. It can support a discrepancy that deserves an answer.
It is never proof of corruption, non-delivery, abandonment, or a percentage of completion.
