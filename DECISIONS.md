# DECISIONS

1. **Deterministic offline-first POC first**: Implemented a static mobile-first PWA shell and deterministic evidence engine before live API integration to maximize demo reliability under low bandwidth.
2. **Strict evidence states over confidence scores**: Used claim-level states (VERIFIED/CORROBORATED/REPORTED/CONFLICTING/UNKNOWN) with explicit provenance metadata.
3. **Verified contact route filtering**: Only verified office routes are returned; unverified personal-like addresses are excluded from action outputs.
4. **Renamed NURU Civic to Wazi**: Kiswahili for "open, clear, plain". *Serikali wazi* is the standing phrase for open government (the primary track) and *sema wazi* means "speak plainly" (the conversation policy). Decisive factor: a product whose evidence model includes UNKNOWN and CONFLICTING cannot be named after light or truth without the name contradicting its own verdicts. Alternatives considered and rejected for collision or fit: Ona (ona.io, a Nairobi civic-data company), Shahidi (too close to Ushahidi), Taarifa (existing platform), Kweli ("truth"). Applied across `package.json`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `service-worker.js` (cache key bumped to `wazi-v1`, which invalidates stale caches), `index.html`, `src/prompts/identity.md` and the docs. Historical references to the former name are deliberately preserved in `DESIGN.md` §1 and Appendix B, and in `README.md`, so the rename remains traceable. Full reasoning: `DESIGN.md` §1.
